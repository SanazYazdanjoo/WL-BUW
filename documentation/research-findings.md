# Preliminary tutor survey findings

**Survey date:** September 2026
**Responses at the time of review:** 4
**Participant group:** Welcome Lounge tutors

These are preliminary, directional findings from a small group of current Welcome Lounge tutors. They are qualitative design input, not representative evidence about all International Office staff, future tutors, or international students. No participant quotations or percentages are reported.

## Repeated questions

Participants generally reported encountering questions that had already been answered somewhere. This supports making common arrival information easier to find and scan.

## Information fragmentation

A strong recurring theme was that information is spread across different places. Students may not know where to look, may miss WhatsApp messages, or may be unsure whether information applies to them.

## Internal continuity

Participants reported occasional loss, duplication, or unclear transfer of information between colleagues. Shared handover context and clear attribution may help tutors pick up ongoing work.

## Existing tools

Respondents generally rated familiar tools such as Excel, WhatsApp, and shared documents as relatively easy to use. The opportunity is to structure and connect existing workflows, not to replace every tool.

## WhatsApp

WhatsApp remains valued as a communication and community channel. The app should make stable information easier to find while leaving conversation, unusual situations, and human support to tutors.

## Maintenance

Responses about semester maintenance were mixed. Maintenance knowledge appears unevenly distributed, so semester preparation should be predictable and documented without assuming that existing processes are universally difficult.

## Design implications

- Centralize navigation to essential arrival information.
- Keep canonical university sources visible and their freshness honest.
- Preserve configured WhatsApp as a route to human support.
- Keep tutor workflows lightweight, attributed, and easy to hand over.
- Retain MasterExcel import/export compatibility.
- Make semester updates reviewable and transferable without code editing.

Do not use these four responses alone to justify student analytics, identity collection, an AI chatbot, complex search, attendance prediction, notifications, or replacement of existing tools. Evaluate those decisions with further evidence and appropriate institutional review.
