# Staff workspace guide

A short, plain guide to the Welcome Lounge staff workspace. Find your role and read that part. Everyone should read **Basics for everyone** first.

| Role | What you do | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tutor** | Help students at the desk, keep their records up to date, write handovers, sign up for shifts | Basics + Tutor |
| **Coordinator** | Everything a tutor does, plus run the semester: tutors, shifts, content, statistics, backups | Basics + Tutor + Coordinator |
| **Admin** | Everything a coordinator does, plus manage logins and see who signed in | All sections |

---

## Basics for everyone

### Signing in

1. Open the staff workspace (`/staff`).
2. Enter your username and password. At the start you use the **shared tutor login**. Ask your coordinator for it.
3. On **Who is working?**, choose your own name. Your name is saved on everything you change.

If your name is not in the list, ask a coordinator to add you.

### Your own login (recommended)

Click your name at the bottom of the menu → **My account** → **Set up your personal login**. Choose a username and a password with at least 8 characters. If you forget it, an Admin can reset it.

### The menu

| Menu item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | Start here. Who is on duty, students who need attention, the latest handover and events |
| **Students** | The student list: search, add, and update students |
| **All Tutors** | Contacts of the program tutors (email, phone, WhatsApp, QR code) |
| **Shifts** | The shift plan. Add yourself to a shift |
| **Handover** | Notes for the next shift |
| **Events** | Events that students see on the student site |

The **Feedback** button (bottom corner) sends a problem or an idea to the Welcome Lounge team. **Online now** at the top shows who else is working.

### Saving

- You do not need to press Save in lists and tables. Changes **save automatically**. Look for **✓ Saved**.
- **Couldn't save** → press **Retry**.
- **Changed elsewhere · Review** → someone else changed the same field. Compare both values and pick **Use mine** or **Use latest**.
- **Offline** banner → Nextcloud can't be reached. Keep working. Your changes are saved when the connection comes back. You can also press **Try now**.

### Privacy rules

- Student data is private. Don't copy it into chats, emails or personal files.
- Write only facts that help the next person in notes. No personal opinions.
- **Sign out** on shared computers (bottom of the menu).

## Tutor guide

### At the start of your shift

1. Open **Today**.
2. Read **Latest handover** to see what the last shift left for you.
3. Look at **Needs attention**. These students still need help.

### When a student comes to the desk

1. Go to **Students** and search by name, matriculation number, country or program.
2. If the student is new, type their name in the **+ New student** row at the bottom of the list and fill in the rest.
3. Update the fields as you help them:
   - **Enrolled**, **Accommodation**, **City registration appointment**
   - **Contact (if no accommodation)** if they don't have a place to stay yet
   - **Needs attention** if something is still open. Untick it when it's solved.
   - **Note** for short, useful facts
4. Wait for **✓ Saved**.

Tip: tick **Needs attention** to see only the open cases. Tick **Show address** only when you need it.

### Before you leave: write a handover

1. Go to **Handover**.
2. Write what the next shift needs to know. Example: "Maria needs a housing contact tomorrow."
3. Choose a student if the note is about one person, or leave it as **General handover**.
4. Press **Add handover**.

### Shifts

Go to **Shifts** and press **+ Add me** on a free place. Press **×** next to your name to remove yourself. You can only change your own name. Ask a coordinator for other changes.

### Events

In **Events** you can add an event (**Add event**), edit it, or remove it. Active events from today onwards appear on the student site.

### Program tutor contacts

**All Tutors** shows who to contact for each study program. Tutors can view this list. Coordinators edit it.

## Coordinator guide

You see everything a tutor sees, plus a **Management** section in the menu. On **Today** you also get summary numbers.

### Before the semester starts

Do these in order:

1. **Semester name**: **Content** → **Semester and support** → enter the semester (for example "WiSe 2026/27"), the WhatsApp group link, and tick **Enable WhatsApp** if needed → **Save settings**.
2. **Staff list**: **Content** → **Staff** → add each person with their role.
3. **Tutor names for shifts**: **Tutors** → **Add tutor** for each tutor → **Save tutor list**. Use ↑ to change the order.
4. **Shift plan**: **Shifts** → **Semester setup** → first day, last day, S1 and S2 times, tutors per shift → **Save semester setup**.
5. **Program tutor contacts**: **All Tutors** → fill in program, tutor, email, phone, WhatsApp link, and upload QR codes.
6. **Student content**: **Content** → **Student content** (see below).
7. **Backup**: **Backup** → **Create backup now**.

### During the semester

| Task | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Change who works which shift | **Shifts**: you can edit every place, not only your own |
| Add or remove a tutor | **Tutors** |
| Check numbers (hours and shifts per tutor, students) | **Statistics** |
| See who changed what, and when | **Change log** |
| Update what students read | **Content** |
| Make a safety copy | **Backup** |

### Editing student content

**Content** → **Student content**. Pick a section: First Step (the student Journey), Useful Info, Student Support, Community or Help.

- **Add** a new item, **Edit** an existing one, or **Remove** it.
- Only **Active** items are shown to students. Make an item inactive to hide it without deleting it.
- The number in front of an item is its order on the student site.
- Changes appear on the student site on the next page load. Open **Student site ↗** (top right) to check.

### Backups

- Go to **Backup** → **Create backup now** before big changes and at least once a week.
- **Download Welcome-Lounge.xlsx** gives you a full copy of the workbook. Keep it only in approved university storage.

### Good habits

- Check **Needs attention** on **Today** every day. Make sure nothing stays open too long.
- Read the **Change log** if something looks wrong.
- At the end of the semester, create a backup and follow the [semester handover](semester-handover.md).

## Admin guide

You see everything a coordinator sees, plus login management.

### Manage logins

Go to **Tutors** and scroll down to **Logins**.

| You want to… | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Help someone who forgot their password | Press **Reset password** next to their name and set a new one. Tell them in person. |
| Give someone a personal login | Press **Set up login** next to their name |
| Send someone back to the shared login | Press **Back to shared login** |
| Change the shared tutor password | Press **Change password** next to the shared login. Tell all tutors. |

You can only give roles up to your own level. For example, an Admin can't make someone a Super Admin.

### See who signed in

Go to **Sign-ins**. It shows who is **Online now** and a list of recent sign-ins. Use it to check for unexpected access.

### Security checklist

- Change the shared tutor password at the start of each semester and when a tutor leaves.
- Remove personal logins of people who have left.
- Never share passwords in group chats. Tell people in person or over an approved channel.
- If you think a login was misused, change the password right away and tell the Super Admin.
